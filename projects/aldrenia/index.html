Aldrenia

a.k.a. Elaria

A third-person action RPG for Minecraft: sword combos, dodges and lock-on, plus an isekai layer of levels, stats and skills you earn by playing.

In development · v0.1.0 Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge Java 21 Blockbench
See it in action What's inside

Two mods, one project

🗡️

Aldrenia Combat

Third-person action combat that works on its own:

  • Combos whose hits follow the animation's real sword path
  • Poise and stagger
  • Dodge rolls with i-frames
  • Procedural walk, run and guard poses
  • Over-the-shoulder camera and lock-on
📜

Aldrenia (RPG)

The isekai RPG layer, built on top of Combat:

  • Levels and EXP
  • 4 stats: STR, AGI, INT, VIT
  • Passive skills unlocked by how you play, defined in JSON
  • HUD and a status window (K)
  • The voice of “the Fragment” that talks to you in chat
