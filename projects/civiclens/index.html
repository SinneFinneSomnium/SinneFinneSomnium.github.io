CivicLens

One map of Thailand. Click a province, then a district, then a subdistrict, and see the government documents and budgets published for it, year by year.

Planned Civic tech Open data Map
Read the idea

The idea

It started from fact-checking Thai news. Government documents and budgets are published online, but they're scattered across many sites and hard to compare. CivicLens would put them in one central place, organized by location.

🇹🇭

Start from the map

A national map you can drill into: จังหวัด → อำเภอ → ตำบล.

💰

Budget per year

For each area, see how much was budgeted in each year.

✍️

Who approved it

Links to the original documents, so anyone can check the source.

Roadmap (draft)

  2. Prototype one province: map + document list
  3. Scale up to the whole country
